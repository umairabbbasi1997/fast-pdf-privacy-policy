Privacy Policy Fast PDF Scanner - Doc Scanner · Effective 18 November 2026 Fast PDF Scanner works entirely on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and your scans are never uploaded to us. This policy explains what the Fast PDF Scanner app (the “App”), published by Zuno (“we”, “us”), does with information when you use it. Information the App handles Scans and documents. Pages you scan or PDFs you create are saved in the App’s private storage on your device. We cannot see them. Photos you choose. If you import from your gallery, the App receives only the photos you select in the system photo picker. It does not browse or read the rest of your library. App settings. Your theme choice (light or dark) and a list of your document names, dates and sizes are stored locally so the App can show your library. What we do not collect We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers or advertising ID. The App has no sign-in, no backend, and no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. Your documents and settings never leave your device unless you share them yourself. How scanning works On Android, scanning uses the Google ML Kit Document Scanner provided through Google Play services. On iOS, it uses Apple’s built-in document camera (VisionKit). Edge detection, cropping and filters are processed on your device. Google and Apple operate these system components under their own privacy policies, and they may handle limited diagnostic or usage information as described there: Google Privacy Policy Apple Privacy Policy Sharing The App shares a document only when you tap Share. It then opens your device’s share sheet so you can choose the app or person to send it to. Once you share a file, the receiving app or service handles it under its own policy. Permissions Camera. Used only by the scanner to capture pages. Images are not sent anywhere by the App. Photos. The App uses the system photo picker, so it does not need broad access to your photos or storage. Storage and deletion You can delete any document inside the App at any time, which permanently removes its file from your device. Uninstalling the App removes all of its data. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover deleted documents. Children The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Changes to this policy If we change how the App handles information, for example by adding a feature that connects to the internet, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
